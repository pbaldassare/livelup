Note:

LivelAPP connects athletes with their Personal Trainer: workout programs, sessions, chat and progress tracking.

Demo accounts (one per role):
- Personal Trainer: pt@demo.it (credentials in the fields above)
- Athlete: atleta@demo.it (same password as the PT account)

The two demo accounts are linked to each other, with sample programs, appointments and chat messages.

There are no purchases in the iOS app. Personal Trainers are business customers and have plans on our website; on iOS there is no pricing, no checkout and no link to buy, and sign-up from the app creates athlete accounts only (free). Trainers sign in with the account created on the website (Guideline 3.1.3(c)).

Location is requested only when the user searches for trainers, events or groups nearby. Camera, photo library and microphone are requested only when the user chooses to upload a photo or a video.

Athletes (the only accounts that can be created in the iOS app) can delete their account in the app: Settings > Elimina account. Any user can also request deletion at https://livelapp.iaconnect.it/elimina-account
