Note:

LivelAPP for iPhone is an app for athletes who train with a Personal Trainer: they follow the workout programs assigned by their trainer, log their sets, track progress, chat with the trainer and find trainers, events and groups nearby.

Changes since the previous submission (Guidelines 3.1.1 and 3.1.3(c)):
- The iOS app no longer gives access to the Personal Trainer area. If a trainer signs in, the app only shows "Area Professionista non disponibile su iPhone" and a sign-out button, with no link to the website or to any purchase.
- Athletes never pay LivelAPP anything: the app is free for them and contains no paid digital content, prices or links to buy. Paid courses are not sold: a trainer can only assign them, and on iOS no price is shown.
- Athletes can send their trainer a request for a training package (in-person or real-time sessions between the athlete and the trainer, Guideline 3.1.3(d)/(e)); there is no payment in the app.

Demo account: atleta@demo.it (credentials in the fields above), linked to a demo trainer with sample programs, appointments and chat messages.

Location is requested only when the user searches for trainers, events or groups nearby. Camera, photo library and microphone are requested only when the user chooses to upload a photo or a video.

Athletes can delete their account in the app: Settings > Elimina account. Anyone can also request deletion at https://livelapp.iaconnect.it/elimina-account
